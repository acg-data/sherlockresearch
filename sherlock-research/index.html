FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Email hello@sherlockreports.com and we reply within one business day.

Who is Sherlock Research for?

Owner-led businesses, franchise teams, local service companies, agencies, and investors who need clear market intelligence without enterprise research overhead. If you're making a pricing, channel, expansion, or acquisition decision in the next 90 days, you're the buyer.

What's actually in a report?

Eight sections: market size and forecast, demand drivers, buyer behavior, competitive landscape, pricing benchmarks, local market cuts, growth opportunities, and an executive playbook. Each section ends with specific recommended actions, not just data.

How is this different from IBISWorld, Statista, or a consultant?

Enterprise research is built for analysts and investors at around $8,000 per seat per year. Consultants run $25K+ for a single project. Sherlock is built for operators at $497 per report, priced and structured to drive specific operational decisions, not committee debate.

Where does the data come from?

Public datasets (U.S. Census Bureau, Bureau of Labor Statistics, trade associations), customer research, and operator input, combined with our own modeling. Every figure is labelled with its sample size and data vintage and tagged as fielded, benchmarked, or modeled, so you always know what you're looking at.

How do I get the report after I pay?

Checkout is handled by Payhip. Available reports download right after payment, and the receipt email includes your download link. Presell reports are emailed the day they're released.

What if it isn't useful?

Email us within 30 days and we'll refund every dollar. If the report doesn't give you at least one insight you can act on, you shouldn't pay for it.
