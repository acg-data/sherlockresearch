FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our reports and pricing.

01What's included in each industry report?

Each report runs 40+ pages covering market size and growth forecasts, competitive landscape, local market breakdowns, pricing benchmarks, and actionable insights — delivered as an instant-download PDF.

02How often are the reports updated?

Reports are refreshed regularly with the latest available data, and each one shows its last-updated date so you always know how current it is. You can also add quarterly or semi-annual update subscriptions to stay ahead.

03What's the difference between quarterly and semi-annual updates?

Quarterly updates give you four comprehensive refreshes per year for maximum currency. Semi-annual updates deliver two in-depth refreshes—at mid-year and year-end—ideal for tracking major market shifts. Both are $199/year and work with any plan.

04Can I purchase more than 5 reports in the Pro Bundle?

Yes. You can add individual reports to any plan, or contact us about Enterprise solutions with unlimited reports and custom research capabilities.

05How will I receive my report?

Instantly. A secure download link is emailed to you the moment your purchase is complete — no waiting and no shipping. Updates are also delivered automatically.

06Can I share the reports with my team?

The Pro Bundle and Enterprise plans include team sharing. Single Report is licensed for individual use.

07Are the reports suitable for business planning?

Absolutely — they're built for it. The forecasts and benchmarks drop straight into strategy, budgeting, and investment decisions. The quarterly or semi-annual updates keep your planning current throughout the year.

08What if I'm not satisfied with my purchase?

Every plan is backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee. If a report isn't right for you, email us for a full refund—no questions asked.

09What do I get with Enterprise?

Enterprise includes unlimited reports, custom research projects, team collaboration, a dedicated account manager, API access, priority support, and SLA guarantees. Contact our sales team to discuss your specific needs.

10Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Yes — you can upgrade anytime, and we'll credit what you've already paid toward the larger bundle. You can also add or remove update subscriptions at any time.
