Key Industry Insights

Storm Cycles

Hail and wind events drive emergency reroofing demand on rolling 5–10 year cycles per market.

Insurance Complexity

Carriers are tightening adjuster oversight; contractors with documentation systems win more approved claims.

Solar Integration

Roof-mounted solar attaches to ~12% of new residential roofs and is reshaping product mix.

Labor Crunch

Skilled crew availability is the #1 constraint on growth across nearly every metro surveyed.

Competitive Landscape

The industry is fragmented, with thousands of small businesses and a mix of regional and national players.
