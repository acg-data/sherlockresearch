General Questions

What is Sherlock Research? +

Sherlock Research provides actionable market intelligence and local insights for small and mid-sized business owners. We deliver clear, data-backed reports that help you understand your market, spot opportunities, and make confident decisions.

Who is Sherlock Research for? +

We serve business owners and operators in industries like landscaping, roofing, HVAC, and pest control. Any business that wants to understand their local market and make smarter decisions can benefit from our research.

How often are reports updated? +

Our core industry reports are updated annually with the latest trends, benchmarks, and market data. We also provide quarterly updates on market highlights and emerging opportunities for active subscribers.

Can I get a custom report? +

Yes! We offer custom research services tailored to your specific market, industry, or competitive questions. Contact our team to discuss your research needs and get a custom proposal.

Pricing & Account

How much does a report cost? +

Our standard industry reports start at $399 and vary based on location and customization. The Pro Bundle includes 5 reports for $799, and Enterprise pricing is custom — contact our team for details.

Is there a subscription required? +

You can purchase reports individually, but we recommend adding quarterly or semi-annual update subscriptions ($199/year) for ongoing access to the latest data. Subscriptions offer better value and keep you ahead of market changes.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee? +

Absolutely. If you're not satisfied with your report within 30 days, we'll refund your full purchase price. We're confident you'll find value in our research.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +

Yes, you can adjust your subscription at any time. Changes take effect in your next billing cycle, and we'll adjust your charges accordingly.
