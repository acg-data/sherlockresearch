About Sherlock
Who is Sherlock Research for?

Owner-led service businesses, franchise teams, local service companies, agencies, and investors. If you're making a pricing, channel, expansion, or acquisition decision in the next 90 days, you're the buyer.

What industries do you cover?

Live today: Landscaping, Roofing, HVAC, and Pest Control. On the roadmap: Cleaning, Dentistry, Fitness, Automotive, and Accounting. If you need a vertical that isn't published yet, email us — we accelerate based on demand.

How is this different from IBISWorld, Statista, or a consultant?

Enterprise research like IBISWorld runs $8K/year per seat and is written for analysts. Consultants charge $25K+ for a single engagement. Sherlock is built for operators: $497–$2,997, structured around decisions you actually need to make, every section ends with a recommended action.

Reports & data
What's actually in a report?

Six modules: demand and market size, buyer behavior, competitive landscape, pricing pressure, channel strategy, and an executive playbook. Each module is built around answering one commercial question and ends with specific recommended actions. The full report is 40+ pages.

How fresh is the data?

The 2026 edition reflects the latest available data, refreshed in May 2026. Quarterly + Coaching subscribers receive a refreshed edition every quarter plus a 1:1 call to apply it.

Where does the data come from?

Four sources cross-referenced: consumer panels (300+ verified service-buying households per market), operator interviews with active business owners, public data anchors (U.S. Census, BLS, association reports), and demand signals (search trends, review velocity, ad-spend indicators). Every number is tagged as simulated, benchmarked, or fielded.

Can I get data for a city not on your list?

We currently cover 22 U.S. metros: Atlanta, Austin, Boston, Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Detroit, Houston, Los Angeles, Miami, Minneapolis, New York, Philadelphia, Phoenix, Riverside, San Antonio, San Diego, San Francisco, Seattle, Tampa, and Washington. If your exact market isn't listed, we'll build the local cut for you within 48 hours at no extra cost.

Pricing & licensing
How much does a report cost?

Standard Report (national) is $497 one-time. City-Specific Report (national + local cut) is $597 one-time. Quarterly + Coaching is $2,000/year and includes four quarterly updates plus four 1:1 calls. All-Access Pass is $2,997 one-time and includes every industry report we publish.

Can I share a report with my team?

Standard and City-Specific reports are licensed for use within a single business or operating company — internal sharing is fine. Quarterly + Coaching includes team sharing within one operating company. All-Access is licensed for use across a team or investment portfolio. For agency or franchise distribution, email us about an enterprise license.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Any one-time purchase credits toward an upgrade within 30 days. Most buyers start with a Standard or City-Specific report, then move to Quarterly + Coaching once they see the value of ongoing data.

Do you offer a refund?

Yes — the 30-day "made a better decision" guarantee. If a report doesn't give you at least one insight you can act on, email us within 30 days for a full refund. We can offer this because operators rarely ask — the data pays for itself on the first decision.

Delivery & format
Is it a real download or a subscription?

Standard, City-Specific, and All-Access are instant PDF downloads, yours to keep and use within your business. Quarterly + Coaching is an annual subscription that includes the downloads plus quarterly refreshes and 1:1 calls.

What format is the report in?

PDF, delivered immediately to your email after purchase. Designed to be readable on screen and printable. The full report runs 40+ pages with charts, benchmarks, and tables.

Do you offer custom research?

Yes. If you need a deeper dive into a specific market, segment, or strategic question that falls outside our standard reports, email hello@sherlockresearch.com and we'll scope a custom engagement. Typical custom work runs $5K–$15K depending on scope.
