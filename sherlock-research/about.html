OUR STORY

Built for operators.
Not analysts.

We believe market research should answer one question: "What do I do Monday?" Everything else is overhead.

WHY WE STARTED SHERLOCK

Enterprise research is too expensive. Anecdotes cost even more.

Small and mid-sized service businesses make seven-figure decisions on hunches. They base pricing on what the competitor down the road charges. They expand into the wrong zip codes. They miss obvious switching demand because no one has put the data in front of them.

The information exists. It's just locked behind $8K enterprise subscriptions designed for analysts at Fortune 500 companies. Or buried in $25K consulting engagements that take 12 weeks to deliver.

Sherlock exists to put operator-grade market intelligence on the table for the price of a one-time decision — not the price of a research department.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Three principles, every report.

01

Action-first, not data-first

Every section ends with a recommended move. If a chart doesn't drive a decision, we cut it.

02

Local matters more than national

03

Affordable on purpose
